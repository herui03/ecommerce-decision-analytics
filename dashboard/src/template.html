E-commerce decision dashboard — independent case study

Two separate studies on public data: a Brazilian online marketplace (orders, deliveries, reviews, payments, seller leads) and a randomised online-advertising experiment. They are never joined: the marketplace data has no treatment, cost or margin fields, and the experiment's features are anonymised. Dataset publishers and licences are under Definitions & sources.
