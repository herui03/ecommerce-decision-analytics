_Generated by `python -m portfolio.pipeline docs` from the committed historical inputs
(prior full-data run, 2026-07-16; recomputed, not re-executed). The dashboard's "Decision memo"

**Bottom line.** Nothing here proves that any intervention would raise revenue. The data supports
three investigations and one budgeting rule, each written as a test to run rather than a gain
already demonstrated.

## Recommendations (investigations and experiments, not promised gains)

1. **Diagnose the late-delivery spikes.** Break the worst months down by seller, carrier region and
   category (needs order-level data), then test one operational change (for example a padded
   delivery estimate or a carrier switch on the worst lanes) in a randomised rollout, measuring the
   late rate and review scores. The monthly co-movement of late deliveries and low reviews is a
   hypothesis until then.
2. **Test freight pricing where it bites.** States with the highest freight share are candidates
   for a freight-subsidy or free-shipping-threshold experiment. The data holds no cost or margin, so
   the experiment must record both before anyone can say a subsidy pays. The extreme (RR) rests
   on 44 orders.
3. **Re-evaluate lead scoring prospectively.** The historical model trained on labels that were not
   complete at its cut (training outcome windows ended 2018-04-01 to
   2018-06-29; the cut was 2018-04-01) and used a look-ahead page-volume
   feature. Retrain with the as-of design and point-in-time features (implemented; demonstrated on
   synthetic data), then run a randomised prioritisation test (score order vs current order) before
   claiming extra wins.
4. **Budget ads on the ITT, not on exposure.** Use the ITT and its interval with real cost and margin
   (the dashboard's break-even calculator shows the arithmetic: each unit of cost per 1,000 assigned
   users needs about 0.868 units of value per incremental conversion). Do not
   budget on the naive exposed-vs-control lift (2,675.83%) or on CACE
   (+3.196 pp, compliers only). Most assigned users were never exposed; find out why before
   scaling.

## What we cannot claim

- Any causal effect of delivery speed, freight or payment method on sales or reviews: the marketplace
  data has no randomised treatment.
- Any ROI: neither dataset holds costs or margins.
- That the reconstructed dbt intermediate layer reproduces the historical numbers: it is validated
  on synthetic data only, because the raw files were not available to this build.
- Why no 2017 lead converted within 90 days: the regime break is observed; its cause is unknown.
- That the 90-day labels were completely observed: the outcome-observation cutoff is an assumption.
- Anything about the real marketplace or ad experiment from the synthetic sample.
